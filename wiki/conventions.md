# Conventions

How Alex wants sessions to behave. `Last-verified: 2026-09-12`

## Communication
- Direct and concise. No filler, no "happy to help". Have opinions; disagree when warranted.
- Be resourceful before asking: read the wiki, read the file, search. Come back with answers.
- Careful with external actions (Slack posts, Jira writes, emails, pushes to shared repos) — ask first.
  Bold with internal ones (reading, organizing, refactoring notes).
- Never send half-baked messages to messaging surfaces. Alex is the voice in Slack/Jira, not the LLM.

## Working style
- Spec-driven, vertical-slice delivery (how stillcasting was built).
- Practical, pattern-questioning learning style (Python, LeetCode).
- Absolute dates everywhere. ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Alex's voice for work text: see [[humanizer-alex]] (skill lives in raw/… Codex bundle; port on demand).

## Wiki hygiene
- One topic per page. Prefer updating an existing page over creating a near-duplicate.
- Double-bracket links resolve by filename (Obsidian-style). Keep slugs unique across the wiki.
- Every project folder with > 2 pages gets its own `index.md`.
- Log entries are short: what changed, why, what's open. Newest at top.
- Old per-project wikis (`~/Documents/ClaudeProjects/*-wiki`) are frozen; this wiki supersedes them.
