## Decision: drop the double period on `/last-survivors` (v1.267.54)
- **Mechanism**: `/last-survivors` is the page we pitch for backlinks ([[backlinks-outreach]]). Its "Last one standing more than once" list closed every sentence
  with a hard-coded `.`, so titles ending in `!`/`?` rendered "… and Them!." (open nit since 10-15). Editors judge a link target by polish. This was the only visible typo on the page.
  The GSC uptick is too small to act on, Google re-crawled nothing, and no bot crosses the Crawl-delay threshold, so there was no stronger SEO lever this run.
- Change: `sentenceEnd()` in `frontend/src/lib/utils.ts` (no period after `.!?…`), used in `last-survivors/page.tsx`. Test `frontend/src/__tests__/last-survivors-sentence-end.test.ts`. Commit a1939c3 (+8/−2 plus test).
- Box tests (self-cleaning containers; clone and images removed): backend **370 passed**; `npm run build` green; full `npx jest`: 10 suites passed incl. the new one,
  **`sc605-search-dropdown.test.tsx` fails 13 tests**. That suite doesn't touch this change, and earlier runs only ran selected suites, which suggests it was already failing (not verified on the previous commit).
  The runbook's deploy gate (backend suite + build) was green.
- First harness attempt died on an SSH idle timeout ("Operation timed out"); the trap cleaned the box. The rerun with `ServerAliveInterval=20` passed. Add keepalives to the harness from now on.
- Deploy: main fast-forwarded, tag **v1.267.54** pushed 10:12 UTC. Box at the tag 10:14, containers recreated 10:17, `/api/health` 200. Pruned 0.81 GB images + 2.0 GB build cache.
  Verified live as Googlebot smartphone: `/last-survivors` 200 `index, follow`, "…The Last Time I Saw Paris and Them!" with no trailing period, no `[!?].` left on the page.
