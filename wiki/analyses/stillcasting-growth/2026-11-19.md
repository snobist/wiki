**The "retracted deaths that keep coming back" mystery from 11-15/11-17 is solved, and the audit itself was the bug.**
- Worker log, 2026-11-18 05:01: `wikidata_dod: person 649384 (Irmelin Krause) DOD confirmed 2026-11-10` and the same for 323888. That is
  `daily_imdb_sync_task` → `wikidata_dod_lookup_task` (`worker/src/stillcasting_worker/tasks.py:197–252`): the IMDb dataset lists both as dead,
  the sync sets `status=deceased` + `death_year`, and the Wikidata lookup **by IMDb id (P345)** writes the exact date. Neither step writes `status_provenance` — that is why 11-17 found "no provenance, no scan".
- Wikidata: Q1672986 (Irmelin Krause) got P570 = 10 Nov 2026 + place of death on 11-11 from an established editor; German Wikipedia says
  "† 10. November 2026 in Berlin-Alt-Hohenschönhausen". Q28100909 (Jorge Montejo) has P570 2026-11-11 plus cause/manner of death added 11-12. Both are real deaths.
- The checker looked Wikidata up **only by TMDb id (P4985)**; neither item has one, so it saw "no Wikidata death", TMDb had dropped (or never kept) the date → `TMDB_RETRACTED`. The 11-15 and 11-17 runs then queued rescans and the scanner's retraction guard **reverted two real deaths** (each flipped back within a day by the IMDb sync). Today: no rescan, both correctly deceased.
- **Tooling fix (wiki only, not prod):** `bin/stillcasting-false-death-check.py` now also selects `imdb_id` and queries Wikidata items matched by TMDb id OR Q-id OR IMDb id (UNION); the two now resolve to Wikidata 2026-11-10 / 2026-11-11 → OK. ## Needs Alex
- **Scanner retraction guard reverts real deaths** (scanner death guards are off-limits for this run): `scan_person`'s "TMDb-only death retracted → revert" rule treats a death as TMDb-only when `status_provenance` has only `tmdb_scan`, but deaths confirmed by the IMDb sync + Wikidata-by-IMDb-id leave no provenance. Suggested fix: (a) `daily_imdb_sync_task` / `wikidata_dod_lookup_task` write a `status_provenance` row (`imdb_dataset` / `wikidata`), and/or (b) the revert checks Wikidata by IMDb id (and `imdb_deaths`) before reverting. Until then, any TMDb-only-provenance death that TMDb drops will flip-flop.
- **It is systemic, not two cases.** Read-only query today: 405 persons have a `tmdb_retraction` provenance row; **398 of them are `deceased` again now** (365 with no exact date — IMDb death year only, e.g. `ramon-vallarino`, `neal-jimenez`, `george-weiss`, `harrison-starr`, `helena-felix`; 33 with a date), **75 were reverted more than once**. Only 6 stayed living (incl. `emmy-williams`, the FUTURE_DATE case). So the guard and the IMDb sync fight each other: each scan flips a real (mostly historic) death to living until the next daily IMDb sync flips it back.
