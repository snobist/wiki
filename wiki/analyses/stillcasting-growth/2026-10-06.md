## Findings
- **Google: no movement.** The sitemap index (submitted 09-24) was downloaded again 10-03 with 0 errors, but nothing new was crawled.
  `/statistics`, `/cause`, `/cause/cancer`, `/spanning-generations`, `/born-in/1950` and 6 of 8 sample titles are still "unknown to Google"
  even though the homepage (re-crawled 10-01) links `/statistics`, `/cause`, `/spanning-generations`. So internal linking is **not** the
  bottleneck — Google is choosing not to follow links (site-level quality/authority verdict). Changes inside the allowed list can't fix that;
  authority (backlinks) and manual "Request indexing" in the GSC UI are the levers, both Alex.
- The broken `sitemap.xml.` submission no longer appears in the GSC sitemaps list (looks deleted).
- **Bing/Yahoo/DDG organic up** (57 → 64 sessions; DDG 7 → 17). Real direct traffic ~US, a few dozen sessions.
- **PerplexityBot burst did not hurt the site**: p95 latency 80 ms for both bot and non-bot traffic during the burst → Crawl-delay would buy nothing measurable and would throttle a citation source. Not done.
- **Bug found: `/og-default.png` returns 404.** It was the fallback og:image for title pages without a poster and person pages without a
  photo, and the `logo` in the About page's Organization JSON-LD. 7,873 PerplexityBot fetches in ~2 h hit it. Broken social cards and a
  404 Organization logo (Google uses it for the logo/knowledge panel) on the only E-E-A-T schema we have.
