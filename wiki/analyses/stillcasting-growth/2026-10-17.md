## Decision: noindex `/born-in/<year>` outside the sitemap range (v1.267.52)
- **Mechanism**: the sitemap lists `/born-in/1900…2010` only (backend `/homepage/sitemap-years` bounds 1900..today−16), but the year page renders
  any year between the DB's min/max birth year (`/born-in/meta` = 1025…2084) as `index, follow`. The hub's year picker (`valid-years`) exposes
  1025, 1077, 1081, 1369, 1678 … and future years, so crawlers reach thin junk pages (stray birth dates) that feed the site-level
  "crawled – currently not indexed" quality verdict. Open item from the 2026-10-08 born-in release. Bing (the only search engine sending
  traffic, +11 this week) crawls everything linked.
- Change: `isIndexableBornYear()` in `frontend/src/lib/sitemap-chunks.ts` mirrors the backend bounds; `born-in/[year]/page.tsx` adds
  `robots: noindex, follow` outside them (pages still render, links still followed). Test `frontend/src/__tests__/born-in-year-indexable.test.ts`. Commit 6192334 (+36/−1).
- Box tests (self-cleaning containers; clone and images removed): backend **365 passed**; frontend jest (new + homepage suites) 5 passed; `npm run build` green.
