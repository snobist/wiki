## Decision: no change this run
- **Google has still not followed the homepage links.** No URL in the sample was re-crawled since 10-23; `/cause/cancer` and `/born-in/1950` are still unknown. Googlebot made 13 requests in 20 h. Another internal-linking tweak would not be seen yet, and it would blur the 10-15/10-23 test.
- **The bottleneck is authority, not on-page.** Eleven runs of small on-site changes have not moved the inspection sample (1 · 11 · 7 since 10-09). The levers that could move it are outside the ALLOWED list: backlinks and IndexNow for Bing (Bing is the only engine sending real traffic).
- No bot crosses the ~5k req/h Crawl-delay threshold (Baidu ≈ 530/h, PerplexityBot ≈ 360/h).
- A deploy would also carry the unreleased tv-player commit on develop.
- Still-open low-value candidate: BreadcrumbList JSON-LD on `/born-in/<year>`.
